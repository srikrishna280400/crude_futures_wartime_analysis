✅ What's Solid

  • Pipeline architecture — 10 scripts, vectorized, compact artifacts, reproducible
  • Statistical hygiene — sample-size discipline, low-confidence flags, ≥3-repetition rule
  • Phase tagging — 6 regimes grounded in web-researched timeline
  • Specific tradable findings:
    • Brent-WTI contemporaneous correlation: 0.725 daily → don't fade intra-day divergence
    • Wednesday EIA days: systematically lower returns in war phases
    • Monday: +1.5% WTI positive bias. Friday: -0.4% bias
    • Phase 4 (naval_blockade_removed): 60% whipsaw_two_sided → range-trade, don't fade

🔴 Critical Gaps

  • NO live regime classifier — can't answer "what phase are we in TODAY?"
  • NO MCX CRUDEOILM data — only WTI/Brent; Indian trader can't directly trade
  • NO backtest — patterns not validated for positive expectancy
  • NO risk management — no stops, targets, or position sizing
  • Small sample — Phase 3 (4 days), Phase 4 (6 days); only 17/1175 triplets have n≥3
  • NO multiple-comparison correction — many "60% patterns" likely spurious

📋 Recommended Next Steps (Priority Order)

🎯 Honest Verdict

Do not trade on this as-is. Use it as research. The historical pattern-mining half is well-executed and reproducible, but converting research → trading requires ~10-12 more weeks of work in 5 specific areas (live classifier, MCX data, backtester, risk management, cross-asset overlay). The current dashboard answers "what has the market historically done in each phase?" — it does not answer "what should I do today?".
